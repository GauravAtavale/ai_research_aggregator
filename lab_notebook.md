# Lab Notebook

A one-line-per-day log of my own work — RL training runs, LLM agent experiments, financial-data
tooling, and this aggregator pipeline itself. This is the highest-value source in the entire COPE
pipeline: no one else can publish it, and it's the concrete, attributable record of original
contribution that matters most for an EB1 authority narrative.

## Rules

- One entry per day, even if it's short. Consistency beats length.
- Log what you tried, what surprised you, and what number moved — not just what you finished.
- Failed experiments and bugs count. A bug that revealed something non-obvious is often better
  pillar-content material than a clean success.
- Link to the artifact (commit, notebook, dataset, chart) whenever there is one.
