# Idea Log

This is the triage output of the daily/weekly digests (`digest_latest.md` and
`weekly_curated_digest_latest.md`). It's the bridge between "things I scanned" and "things worth
writing about."

## Triage Filter

When skimming a digest, an item goes in this log only if it passes at least one of:

1. **Contradicts a common belief** — challenges something widely assumed in RL, LLMs, or applied AI.
2. **Applies directly to my own work** — RL evals, LLM agents, or financial-data tooling.
3. **Has a striking number or result** — something quantifiable and surprising enough to anchor a post.

If none apply, skip it — don't log it just because it's new or trending.

## Status Definitions

- **New** — logged, not yet reviewed for writing potential.
- **Candidate** — reviewed, worth writing about, not yet started.
- **Drafting** — actively being turned into a post.
- **Published** — written and shared.
- **Skipped** — reviewed and decided against, with a one-line reason (keeps the log honest and
  prevents re-litigating the same item later).
